Two measures per ZIP

The two ownership columns
  • Rate · 55.9%Homeownership rate — owner-occupied homes ÷ occupied homes. The standard tenure measure.

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025). Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage1,803 populated California ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. “$250,001” means “$250,000 or more” (Census top-code). Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesSupremeChoice-CA-ZIP-Demographics-ACS-2020-2024 (.xlsx and .csv) — every ZIP, every column, with filter flags. Companion: SupremeChoice-CA-ZIP-Brush-Fire-Risk-2026.
