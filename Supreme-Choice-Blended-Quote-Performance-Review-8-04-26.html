Data notes

Sources and conventions

Window. Every figure covers 2026 year to date through August 4, 2026. Dispositions were last imported July 15, 2026.

Quote rate is quoted divided by leads delivered. Contact-to-quote is quoted divided by contacted. Quote-to-close is policies divided by quotes. All three are recomputed from the counts in each export; the conversion-rate columns in the platform exports compute off clicks and are not used.

Minimum volume threshold. Rankings include only groups with at least 10 leads year to date. A small number of low-volume groups return more matched dispositions than leads attributed to that group; those groups sit below the threshold and are excluded from every ranking.

Contact-to-quote on Home. Home records 306 contacts and 198 quotes year to date, a contact-to-quote rate of 64.7%. The rate sits between 59% and 71% on every Home source, so it does not track any single source. Auto records 87 quotes against 92 contacts, 94.6%. The Home gap of 108 records is carried as an open data question against the disposition mapping, where some statuses map to contacted without mapping to quoted.

Blended views. County, source and age rows on this page combine Home and Auto. Because contact-to-quote differs sharply between the two campaigns, blended contact-to-quote and quote rate read as a volume-weighted mix of the two; per-campaign figures appear in section 03 and in the per-campaign reports.
