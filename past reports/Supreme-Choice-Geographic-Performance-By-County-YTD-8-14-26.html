07

Data sources and coverage

How the county and ZIP figures are produced

County figures carry cost; ZIP figures do not
County leads, cost, contacts, quotes and policies come from the platform county export, which covers the full year-to-date window. ZIP codes are not available in that export. The ZIP figures come from the prospect list, the only export carrying a ZIP field: it holds 1,072 records for the window against the platform's 1,070 leads, every record carries a ZIP, and rolling those ZIPs up to county matches the platform county lead counts within two leads across every county.
Three limits on the ZIP tables
No cost field exists in the prospect list, so cost per policy is not shown by ZIP. Quote and contact flags in that list cover 34 quotes and 76 contacts against the platform's 285 and 403, so quote and contact rates are not shown by ZIP. 19 of the 23 policies recorded before Aug 14 carry a ZIP; with the Aug 14 addition that is 20 of 24. County-level figures in sections 02 through 04 are unaffected by all three limits.

Recommendations

1
Weight budget toward the four counties above a 4% policy rate
San Joaquin, Contra Costa, Orange and Riverside produce 12 of 24 policies from 228 leads and $1,331.83, at $77.56, $63.55, $107.48 and $145.44 per policy against $279.43 for the account.
2
Review Los Angeles
Los Angeles carries 281 leads and $2,204.43 — 32.9% of media cost — and closes 6 policies at $367.41, on a 34.88% contact rate. It is the largest single line item in the account.
3
Split the zero-policy counties by contact rate before acting
Sacramento, Alameda and Stanislaus carry 74 leads at contact rates of 48.6%, 46.2% and 38.5% with no close. Santa Clara, Shasta, Ventura and Butte carry 77 leads at contact rates between 4.2% and 10.5%. The first group is a closing question; the second is a working question.
4
Confirm the four policies missing a ZIP
One policy each in Los Angeles, Riverside, San Joaquin and Kern appears in the platform county totals but carries no matching prospect record with a ZIP.
5
Review the highest-volume ZIP codes without a close
92545 Hemet, 90266 Manhattan Beach, 91750 La Verne, 92260 Palm Desert, 92530 Lake Elsinore and 92543 Hemet carry 53 leads between them and no policy.
