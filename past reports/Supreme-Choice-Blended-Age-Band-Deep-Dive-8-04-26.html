08

Data notes

Sources and conventions

Window. Every figure covers 2026 year to date through August 4, 2026. Dispositions were last imported July 15, 2026.

Contact rate is contacted divided by leads delivered, recomputed from the lead counts in each export. The conversion-rate columns in the platform exports compute off clicks; every rate here is recomputed off leads.

Quoted equals contacted for this account, since every contact is quoted. The two managed measures are contact rate and quote-to-close.

Age coverage. Consumer age is exported as single years and grouped into bands here. 1,028 leads carry an age against 1,028 delivered across both campaigns year to date.

Invalid age codes. The Home export returns a value of 125, which falls outside a valid age range. It carries 13 leads and 4 contacts and is grouped as unknown, excluded from every band above.

Blended view. Bands on this page combine Home and Auto so the age curve reads across the whole account. Per-campaign age reads appear in section 06, and campaign health is judged on the per-campaign reports.
