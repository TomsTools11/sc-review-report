Reading note

How the conversion columns are timestamped

Lead counts are timestamped at delivery; quote and contact counts are timestamped when the action is logged
A lead delivered at 9 AM and quoted at 1 PM is counted in the 9 AM lead figure and the 1 PM quote figure. Two cells — Friday 1 PM (11 quotes on 5 leads) and Wednesday 1 PM (9 quotes on 7 leads) — record more quotes than leads for this reason. Hourly lead, cost, position and win-rate figures describe when traffic arrives; hourly quote and contact figures describe when the agency works it.
