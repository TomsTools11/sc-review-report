Scoring system

Public federal data, expressed on the commercial scales

The four commercial models mirrored
  • FireLineVerisk (formerly ISO) · 0–30 scale: 0–1 low, 2–3 moderate, 4–12 high, 13–30 extreme. Slope × fuel + access.
  • Wildfire Risk ScoreCotality (formerly CoreLogic) · 1–100: satellite-based burn probability, fuel density, ember exposure.
  • Z-FIREZesty.ai · 1–10: AI on aerial imagery — roof material, vegetation, defensible space.
  • PPCISO · 1–10: community fire protection — department capability, water supply, dispatch.
  • AvailabilityAll four are licensed at the property level and not published by ZIP. The composite below is built from public federal data on the same recipe and reported on all three scales (0–100, 1–10, FireLine-style 0–30).

Tier rules
  • ExtremeHousing-weighted NRI score 95+, or 50%+ of the ZIP's housing in high-risk tracts
  • HighScore 80+, or 20%+ of housing in high-risk tracts
  • ModerateScore 45+, 5%+ of housing in high-risk tracts, or any single tract at 99+
  • LowEverything else
Reference points
  • Known fire ZIPsParadise 95969, Malibu 90265, Topanga 90290, Lake Arrowhead 92352, Idyllwild 92549, Julian 92036 — all score Extreme
  • Hillside blendsBeverly Hills 90210 scores 51.3 (High); Santa Rosa 95404 scores 49.2 (High); Oakland 94611 scores 30.9 (Moderate, worst tract 97)
  • Urban coresSan Francisco 94103 scores 0.9; downtown Los Angeles 90012 scores 18.9; downtown San Diego 92101 scores 15.4 — all Low

Data & files

  • SourcesFEMA National Risk Index (December 2025), 9,106 CA census tracts, crosswalked to 2020 ZCTAs; U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2020–2024 (housing weights, demographics).
  • ScopeA campaign-geography instrument. Carriers score individual parcels with the commercial models above, and CAL FIRE Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps (updated February–March 2025) carry the regulatory designations; a Low ZIP can contain higher-risk pockets, flagged in the worst-tract column.
  • FilesSupremeChoice-CA-ZIP-Brush-Fire-Risk-2026 (.xlsx and .csv) — every ZIP with composite score, tier, all three scales, components, and the exclusion-candidate column. Companion: SupremeChoice-CA-ZIP-Demographics-ACS-2020-2024.
